Step 1 · The prompt

Create your avatar

Two clear photos, one prompt, your honest game-character starting point.

Create a stylized 3D game-character avatar of the person in these photos. Use the face photo for their face — keep it clearly recognizable (same face shape, eyes, hairstyle, skin tone). Use the body photo for their CURRENT physique — be honest and realistic about their present body, no idealized muscles, show them as they actually are now. Athletic-style standing pose, dramatic game-key-art lighting, dark background with subtle rim light. Full body visible. Video game character style, NOT photorealistic, NOT cartoon.

Brutal truth: the avatar starts from your real body today. As you train, it evolves.

Photos that work best

  • Face photo: front-facing, good light, no sunglasses or hats.
  • Full-body photo: head to feet, plain background, fitted clothes.
  • Both photos stay on this phone — nothing uploads until you share.
